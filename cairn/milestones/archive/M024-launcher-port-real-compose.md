**Status:** done (2026-11-05, PR #35 https://github.com/jmgirard/rstudio2u/pull/35)

**Goal:** CI runs `start_linux.sh` against real Docker and Compose and fails when the port the launcher announces is not the port that answers.

**Outcome:** Internal: no user-visible change and no release. `scripts/tests/posix/run_launcher_real_compose.sh` runs the launcher from a temporary copy under Compose project `rstudio2u-real-compose-test` with the published image. Nine `.env` contents compare `launcher_requested_port` with `docker compose config`. `launcher-compose` jobs: `launchers.yml` on pull requests, with `docker-compose.yml` added to `paths`; `docker.yml` after `publish` on schedule and dispatch, in `notify`'s `needs` and `RESULTS`. Both have `timeout-minutes: 20`. The comment at `launcher_common.sh:167` now records that Compose 5.6.1 exits 1 with `no port 8787/tcp`. DESIGN gained a Known issues line (Linux launcher, amd64, runner's Compose only) and a note that a failed job counts against the rebuild gap check.
